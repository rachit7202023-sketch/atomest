## Outreach guidelines

- **Send fewer, better messages.** 5 personalized emails per week beats 500 generic ones.
- **Reference something specific** about their business (their site, a review, a service they offer).
- **No attachments** in the first email (spam filter risk).
- **One clear CTA** (a 15–20 minute call).
- **Follow up once** 3–4 days later if no reply. Stop after 2 attempts.
- **Honor opt-outs immediately.** If someone says not to contact them, remove them from your list.
- **Never fabricate** a connection ("I spoke with your colleague" when you didn't).

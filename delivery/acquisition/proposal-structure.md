# Proposal Structure

**Purpose:** Standard structure for Atomest service proposals.  
**Principle:** Transparent, honest, no fabricated guarantees. Every proposal is customized to the client.

---

## 1. Cover

- Atomest branding
- Client name and business
- Proposal date
- Prepared by (Atomest contact)
- Service division(s) proposed

## 2. Executive Summary

- Brief restatement of the client's challenge (in their own words from discovery)
- High-level solution overview
- Why Atomest is a fit

## 3. Scope of Work

What is included:
- Specific deliverables (e.g., "AI voice receptionist configured with 2 FAQs and calendar booking")
- Number of revisions/rounds
- Integrations included (CRM, calendar, email, etc.)
- Training/handoff sessions

What is NOT included (important for scope discipline):
- Client's phone numbers, telephony service, or carrier fees
- Client's LLM API usage fees (Gemini, ElevenLabs, etc.)
- Client's CRM or software subscription fees
- Content creation (copywriting, photography)
- 24/7 monitoring unless a separate maintenance agreement is signed

## 4. Timeline

| Phase | Activity | Estimated Duration |
|-------|----------|-------------------|
| Discovery & requirements | Confirm systems, content, access | 1 week |
| Build & configure | Set up workflows, integrations, knowledge base | 2–3 weeks |
| Testing | End-to-end testing with client data | 1 week |
| Launch | Cut over, go live | 1 week |
| Handoff | Documentation, training, credentials | 1 week |

## 5. Investment

| Item | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Setup & implementation | $X,XXX |
| First month monitoring/maintenance | $XXX |
| Total (first month) | $X,XXX |
| Ongoing monthly maintenance (optional) | $XXX/month |

Note: Third-party costs (telephony, LLM API, CRM subscriptions) are billed directly to the client by their providers and are not included above.

## 6. Assumptions & Dependencies

- Client provides phone number/DID (for voice)
- Client provides LLM API credentials
- Client provides calendar/CRM access (OAuth/API)
- Client provides knowledge base content (FAQs, docs)
- Client reviews and approves configuration before launch

## 7. Terms

- Payment schedule (e.g., 50% deposit, 50% on launch)
- Change order process
- Warranty period (e.g., 30 days post-launch for bug fixes)
- Termination clause
- Atomest does not guarantee specific lead volumes, revenue increases, or call answer rates

## 8. Acceptance

- Signature line for client
- Signature line for Atomest
- Date

---

## What we do NOT promise (be explicit)

- "Guaranteed #1 Google ranking"
- "Guaranteed X leads per month"
- "100% automated with no human oversight"
- Fake testimonials or client logos
- Ownership of client phone numbers or domains

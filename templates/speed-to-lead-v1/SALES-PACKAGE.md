## 1. ONE-PAGE SERVICE DESCRIPTION

### The Problem
When a qualified prospect fills out a website contact form, they are in peak buying mode. Most boutique marketing agencies and web studios take **2 to 8 hours** (or next business day) to respond because leads sit in an unmonitored inbox. Studies show responding within 15 minutes increases qualification rates by **over 300%**. Delayed responses cost thousands in lost project revenue.

### The Solution: Speed-to-Lead Inbound Engine
A bulletproof, production-grade automation that bridges your website form to your team's instant communication channels in under **45 seconds**:
1. **Instant Webhook Ingestion**: Works with WordPress, Webflow, Framer, Squarespace, or custom forms.
2. **Schema Validation & Spam Honeypot**: Rejects bots and malformed emails before they reach your team.
3. **Structured CRM & Google Sheets Sync**: Auto-creates or updates client records with zero manual data entry.
4. **Immediate Telegram / Slack Alert**: Pings your phone with a structured briefing (Name, Company, Scope, SLA countdown).
5. **Dynamic Prospect Acknowledgment**: Delivers an immediate, professional confirmation email setting expectations.

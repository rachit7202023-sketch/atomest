## 1. THE TARGET PROFILE (WHO TO PITCH)

Do NOT pitch giant tech companies or enterprise leaders. Pitch local, regional, or boutique businesses where the **founder, owner, or partner** makes quick purchasing decisions:

1. **Boutique Professional Services:** Accounting firms, legal consultancies, tax advisors, wealth planners.
2. **High-Ticket Local Contractors & Home Services:** Custom home builders, luxury interior designers, architectural studios, solar/HVAC installers.
3. **Medical & Health Clinics:** Aesthetic dental clinics, physiotherapy studios, private cosmetic clinics.
4. **B2B Agencies & Consultancies:** Recruiting firms, small PR/marketing agencies whose own websites haven't been updated since 2018.

---

## 2. HOW TO FIND 30 QUALIFIED TARGETS IN 2 HOURS

1. Go to Google Maps or Google Search in your target city (or target English-speaking metro: e.g., London, Melbourne, Austin, Dubai, Mumbai, Bangalore).
2. Search: `[Industry] in [City]` (e.g., `commercial interior design in Melbourne` or `accounting firm in Austin`).
3. Click through to their websites and look for **red flags**:
   - Takes 4+ seconds to load.
   - Looks terrible or hard to read on mobile.
   - Has a generic `mailto:` link or a broken contact form.
   - Copyright year in footer says `2019` or `2021`.
4. Note down the Founder/Managing Partner's name and email in a Google Sheet.

## 3. THE "2-MINUTE LOOM AUDIT" (THE HIGHEST CONVERTING METHOD)

Never send generic text spam. A personalized screen-share video gets 5x higher response rates because it proves you actually did real work before reaching out.

### The 2-Minute Video Structure:
* **0:00 - 0:20 (Genuine Compliment):** *"Hi [Name], was researching top [Industry] firms in [City] and came across [Company]. Really impressed by [specific recent project or portfolio item]."*
* **0:20 - 1:00 (The Opportunity / Problem):** *"I pulled up your site on my phone and noticed that your consultation booking form takes about 5 seconds to respond, and on smaller screens, the input fields get pushed below the fold. When you're running ads or getting referrals, that friction typically costs you 20-30% of your incoming inquiries."*
* **1:00 - 1:40 (Show, Don't Just Tell):** *"We build modern, fast web platforms and speed-to-lead systems at Atomest. Here is a quick layout of how sub-second mobile loading looks and how inquiries can instantly notify your team on Slack or WhatsApp in under 60 seconds."*
* **1:40 - 2:00 (Low-Pressure CTA):** *"No high-pressure sales pitch at all—just thought this might be helpful feedback. If you'd like me to send over a few notes on how to fix that form or want a quick chat, let me know! Either way, keep up the great work with [Company]."*
